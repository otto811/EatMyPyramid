Your Profile & Setup

This program is set up to track your daily food intake one week at a time. Each week is saved and can be recalled using the recall a week Button. You can track your results by macros and energy food group servings, original EMP points or all three.

(EatMyPyramid v7.0)
Macro targets (Protein, Fat, Carbs) are tracked as a percentage of your daily calories. Data saves automatically.

Search & Log Food

Searches are conducted using the databases you select from the 'Databases' button. You will need to load API keys for the USDA Foodcentral and Gemini Ai meal search function. This is a one time process. Instructions to obtain the keys are given when you press those buttons, and it is very easy to do. There are multiple ways to search for items. You can do it one at a time using the search field or use the barcode button to scan an item’s barcode. You can enter multiple items at a time manually, use AI text parsing or even upload a picture from your camera all using the ‘Search a Meal’ button.
Nutritional information for the selected item in Search Results field.
Choose "Do not count" for items like snacks, drinks, or condiments that shouldn't increment group totals. Water target is 8 cups/day by default.

Macros & Energy

Food Groups (Servings)

This section is to help you focus on eating Whole Foods or items that contain a high percentage of a food group. If the item is logged and pre-assigned to a food group, you'll see it here. You can manually bump the bars up or down 1/2 servings at a time if a food assignment was missed. Set your goals for the number of servings you would like to eat in a day in the small boxes on the right.

Daily Servings Benchmark (a standard 2,000-calorie daily profile breaks down into these specific group targets:
• Vegetables: 3 servings per day.
• Fruits: 2 servings per day.
• Protein Foods: 1.2 to 1.6 grams per kilogram of body weight daily.
• Dairy: 3 servings per day.
• Whole Grains: 2 to 4 servings per day.

The core recommendation is to focus exclusively on fiber-rich options (oats, brown rice, quinoa) while significantly cutting back on refined flours. A single serving equals 1 slice of whole-wheat bread or 1/2 cup of cooked grains or pasta. You can explore the interactive calculators, download full PDF tracking worksheets, and pull up your personal serving targets directly by visiting the primary resource at RealFood.gov.

Manage My Foods

My Foods is a local database you create. It allows you to customize based on the foods you consume often. You can create an item from scratch, add a scanned or searched item, fix bad info from online items, modify nutrition info based on preferred serving sizes. All searches will place items from 'My Foods' at the top of the list.

📅 Recall a Week's Tracking History

Pick a date (or use the dropdown) from any past week. The Day selector, Daily Log, Macros, Food Groups, and EMP Points will switch to show that week's saved data for browsing.

Tip: While viewing a past week you can browse every day but logging, editing, exercise points, and resets are disabled (to protect your history). Click the ← Return to Live button (appears when active) or re-select the current week to resume normal tracking.
